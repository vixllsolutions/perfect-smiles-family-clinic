A LITTLE ABOUT US

Dental care with a human touch.

At Perfect Smiles Family Dental Care, we believe good dentistry starts with listening. Our aim is to help you understand your options and feel supported throughout your dental visit.

Led by Dr. Prabha S. Shivalli, the clinic offers general, restorative and cosmetic dental services for individuals and families in Nagarbhavi, Bengaluru.
